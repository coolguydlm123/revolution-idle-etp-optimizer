Tier, Tetr and Pent your Rank unlocks

Reach an atom mastery multiplier (odd A)

Pick an odd A node and the multiplier you want. It finds the cheapest way to get there from your tree levels, and how much ETP that takes.

Spark rarity

Spark Luck comes from A14. Only luck sets the drop chances (Spark Mastery doesn't). Between the breakpoints 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 30 … 100, each rarity's chance changes linearly.

ETP needed for a Spark Luck target

Spark Luck is A14's effect. It finds the cheapest way to reach the target from your tree levels (ε → D2 → C4 → B7 → A14), with your bonuses and max level. Luck can't reach 100.

Luck needed for a rarity drop rate

Pick a rarity and the drop rate you want. It finds the lowest Spark Luck that gives that rate, then the ETP to reach it.

Atoms to Singularity

Atoms are entered as exponents (log10), so 1e150 atoms is 150. Every second atoms are multiplied by the atoms gain, so on a log scale the exponent grows by log10(gain) × speed per second. Past each atom threshold a penalty raises the gain to the power 1 / divisor, which divides that growth.
