What it assumes

All nodes are unlocked (no level 5 requirement). The formulas were read from the game's code: level soft caps, then efficiency is multiplied by the effect of the next node toward ε (only if that node has at least one level), then the node's own effect curve. The efficiency multiplier is the Pent milestone 10 bonus, 1 + 0.01 × Pent, applied to every node's efficiency (only from 10 Pent). It matched your game at Pent 13 (×1.13) and Pent 16 (×1.16). Other efficiency bonuses I found in the code (milestone-style fields) are not modelled. Node-specific extras on A14 and A16 (ids 28 and 30) from certain upgrades are not modelled. Results come from a numeric search, so levels can be off by a few from the true optimum.
